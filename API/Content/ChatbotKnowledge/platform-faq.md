# GreenBeauty Platform FAQ

## Marketplace overview
GreenBeauty is a natural beauty marketplace where customers can discover cosmetics and raw cosmetic ingredients from verified sellers. The platform includes products, seller profiles, product reviews, favorites, cart, checkout, order tracking, messaging and educational blog content.

## Product discovery
Customers can browse all products from Categories > All Products or filter by Cosmetics and Ingredients. Product pages include images, price, seller, description, product details, reviews and intelligent suggestions based on product name, category, benefits, usage area, formula and skin type.

## Favorites
Logged-in customers can save products to Favorites. Favorites are available from the header navigation and are meant for products the user wants to revisit later.

## Seller pages
Every verified seller can have a public seller page with company name, story, history, location, map, accreditation documents and available products. Customers can open a seller page by clicking the seller name on a product page.
