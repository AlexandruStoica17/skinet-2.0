# Orders and Delivery

## Placing an order
Customers add products to the basket, review the basket, choose delivery details and complete checkout. The basket icon in the header shows how many items are currently in the basket.

## Order status
Customers can view their orders from My Orders. A seller may update the order status, for example from Pending to Shipped. Order-specific information should only be shown to the authenticated customer who owns that order.

## Delivery updates
When a seller updates an order status, the customer can receive a notification in the application. If the status is Shipped, the customer should check My Orders for the latest delivery information.

## Support for order problems
For payment, delivery or incorrect-product problems, the customer should use My Orders and contact the seller or platform support. The chatbot should not ask for card details, passwords or private payment information.
