# Sellers and Products

## Contacting a seller
On a product page, customers can use Contact Seller to open a direct conversation with the seller. If a previous direct conversation exists, the platform reuses it; otherwise, it creates a new direct conversation.

## Cosmetics sellers
Cosmetics sellers publish finished skincare products such as cleansers, creams, masks, soaps, gels and care formulas. Product information may include skin type, usage area, benefits and formula or texture.

## Ingredient sellers
Ingredient sellers publish cosmetic raw materials such as aloe vera, dried flowers, honey, mint, shea butter, botanical extracts and cosmetic bases. Ingredient product pages should clearly describe usage area, benefits, texture and seller origin.

## Product recommendations
Recommendations should be semantically related to the viewed product. Similarity may use product category, ingredients, benefits, usage area, formula, skin type, seller and keywords. The system should avoid random suggestions that share no meaningful relation.
